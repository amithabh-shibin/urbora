What we do

Urbora Technical Services and Maintenance L.L.C – S.P.C provides technical contracting, interior décor implementation and building maintenance. Our scope includes electrical fittings, air conditioning and ventilation, plumbing and plastering.

From a fixture replacement to a multi-trade fit-out, identifying the property conditions, access and compatible finishes early helps define the work and the order it should happen in.
