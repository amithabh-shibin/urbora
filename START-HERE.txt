URBORA WEBSITE

Preview
Unzip the package and open index.html, or serve the extracted folder using a static host.
Upload the contents of the extracted folder to the document root of your hosting account.

Contact setup
The phone and WhatsApp numbers are set to +971 52 377 0110. Update them or add an email
in config.js as needed. Use full international numbers. The floating WhatsApp icon appears
at every screen width once configured. The floating call icon appears up to 1024px.
The enquiry form prepares a message for WhatsApp or email and displays a copyable draft.
It is a static form and has no server-side submission or storage.

SEO setup
Replace https://example.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with the live HTTPS domain.
Each page has a distinct title and description plus social preview text. After publishing,
submit the live sitemap URL in Google Search Console.

Images and testimonials
The website uses illustrative generated images and a text-based brand mark based on the
supplied green and charcoal branding. Replace the illustrative testimonial cards and portraits
with approved customer reviews and photos before presenting them as customer testimonials.
