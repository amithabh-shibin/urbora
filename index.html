FAQ

Common questions

What services does Urbora offer?

Urbora covers interior décor works, electrical contracting and fixtures, building maintenance, air conditioning and ventilation, plumbing and plastering.

Can I include several services in one enquiry?

Yes. List everything you need, including related tasks such as electrical fittings, plaster patching or plumbing.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Share the location, property type, a short scope, any photos or drawings you have and your preferred dates.

Do you handle both installation and maintenance?

Yes. Installation and maintenance are part of the listed service scope. Include the equipment or fittings involved so Urbora can review your request.

Can I request work for a commercial property?

Use the enquiry page to describe the premises, access hours and any building management requirements.
